01 / The workshop

Intelligence that moves
with the physical world.

Physical AI is bringing perception, reasoning, and action closer together. Real-world robots must do all three within practical energy, latency, memory, and communication budgets.

This proposed workshop brings the neuromorphic and robotics communities together to explore how event-driven sensing and sparse computation can enable responsive, edge-efficient embodied intelligence.

01 —

Sense differently

Explore event-driven and multimodal sensing for responsive robotic perception.

02 —

Compute efficiently

Connect spiking and hybrid learning methods with neuromorphic hardware and deployment.

03 —

Measure what matters

Evaluate task performance, energy, latency, and closed-loop behavior across the perception–action loop.

04 / Call for contributions

Bring your perspective
to the conversation.

The proposed open call welcomes original research, preliminary results, position papers, benchmarks, and emerging research directions across the workshop themes.

Submissions are planned as 4–8 page papers through OpenReview, with three reviews per paper under a single-blind process. Selected contributions will be presented as oral spotlights, posters, or demonstrations.

Submission details and dates are tentative and may change with the workshop schedule.

Submission deadlineMarch 31, 2027
